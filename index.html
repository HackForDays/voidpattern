Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VoidPattern ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website, digital products, and services including designs sold on Redbubble and digital downloads sold on Gumroad.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information on this site. When you purchase our products through third-party platforms (Gumroad, Redbubble), those platforms collect and process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Gumroad Privacy Policy
  • Redbubble Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Information

We use the Pinterest API to share our creative work (generative art and design products) on Pinterest. Through this integration, we access only the permissions required to post content to our own Pinterest boards. We do not access, store, or share your Pinterest account data.

3. Pinterest API Usage

Our application uses the Pinterest API solely to:

  • Create and manage our own Pinterest boards
  • Pin our own original artwork and product links to those boards
  • Read our own account information to verify connectivity

We do not collect, store, or share any data belonging to Pinterest users other than our own account.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our products and links may direct you to third-party platforms (Redbubble, Gumroad). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

6. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. All purchase and user data is handled exclusively by the respective third-party platforms.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data including access, correction, deletion, and portability. For data held by third-party platforms, please refer to their respective privacy policies and support channels.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
